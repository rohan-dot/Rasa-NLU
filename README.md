

**CAITT**

- Designed and implemented an agentic RAG-based Q&A system that performs iterative, multi-step retrieval over chat logs — rather than single-shot retrieval — producing more accurate and better-grounded responses, paired with a judge LLM for automated evaluation. Currently applying this agentic approach to a 618 AOC mission planner operational use case for real-world evaluation. (Technical)

- Developed an LLM-powered mission planning prototype in support of the 618 use case: a closed-world extraction pipeline that pulls verbatim, provenance-checked clearance data (diplomatic clearance lead times, overflight permissions, customs, HAZMAT, airfield restrictions) from Foreign Clearance Guide country files and fuses it with enroute support remarks, airfield detail databases, and NOTAM data against a structured 91-item mission checklist. (Technical)

- Built an agentic flight route planner on top of the extracted data, combining graph-based multi-stop routing, overflight corridor analysis, LLM-based feasibility adjudication with structured verdicts, DLA fuel cost estimation, and live weather (METAR/TAF) and air traffic overlays — demonstrating end-to-end compliant route planning for the operational use case. (Technical)

- Presented the Q&A / RAG system and judge LLM to transition partner and stakeholders during weekly program meetings to provide research updates and solicit feedback. (Stakeholder engagement)

- Extended the system to operate on open-source data (BioASQ) to further validate and benchmark performance; participated in the CLEF 2026 BioASQ challenge. (Technical / Publication)

- CAITT agentic RAG research accepted and presented at KDD 2026, Agent4IR Workshop, and at the CLEF 2026 BioASQ Workshop. (Publication)

- Co-chaired a session at the MORS 94th Symposium, presented the Q&A / RAG system to two working groups, and delivered an accepted talk on semantic search using agentic AI at two workshops under the main symposium. (Presentation / Sponsor/External engagement)

- Delivered a tutorial at RAAINS and CTNS on agentic AI and its practical applications. (External engagement)
